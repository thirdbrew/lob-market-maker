# Notices

## Not investment advice

This repository is a research and engineering exercise. The order flow and both
market-making agents were written by the same author, so any profit or loss
reported here is a property of those assumptions and is **not** evidence of an
edge in any real market. The simulator is not calibrated to any instrument.

Nothing here is a recommendation to trade.

## What the results are about

The headline finding — that a naive fixed spread dominates the Avellaneda–Stoikov
closed form on this frontier — is a statement about **this simulator**, in which
35% of market orders are informed. It is not a refutation of Avellaneda & Stoikov
(2008), whose derivation contains no informed traders at all. See the README.
